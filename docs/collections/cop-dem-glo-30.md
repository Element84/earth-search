# Copernicus DEM 30m (cop-dem-glo-30)

- Collection: [`cop-dem-glo-30`](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/cop-dem-glo-30)
- Public dataset: [Copernicus DEM](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/)
- stactools package: [cop-dem](https://github.com/stactools-packages/cop-dem)
- Number of Items: ~ 26,000

The Copernicus DEM data is available in two different Collections with the only difference being the
resolution: 30 meter vs 90 meter. See also [`cop-dem-glo-90`](cop-dem-glo-90.md).
